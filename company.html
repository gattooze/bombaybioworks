Who we are

Built on
solid ground.

We are a small team working on a large problem. Converting biological material into carbon products that remove carbon from the atmosphere, permanently, while doing useful work in the world.

Our conviction

Why this.
Why now.

Carbon removal needs to be real

Most climate commitments rely on avoided emissions — not actual removal. The world needs a parallel infrastructure of physical carbon removal: verifiable, permanent, and scalable. Thermochemical conversion of biological material is one of the most credible pathways to get there. We are building in that space.

Products that pay for themselves

Carbon removal should not need to be subsidised indefinitely. The carbon we produce has genuine industrial utility — it improves soil, powers furnaces, and purifies water. This commercial demand means the economics work without waiting for carbon credit markets to mature. Removal happens as a consequence of doing something valuable.

India has the feedstock and the demand

India generates hundreds of millions of tonnes of agricultural residue annually. Industries are under increasing pressure to reduce fossil fuel dependence. The agriculture sector needs better soil solutions. The raw material and the markets are both here. We are building the conversion infrastructure between them.

"We do not think of carbon as a problem to solve. We think of it as a material to work with — one that, handled correctly, can leave the world better than we found it."

— Bombay Bioworks founding position

What we believe

Four
principles.

01
Permanence over performance

A tonne of carbon that stays removed for five hundred years is worth more than one that comes back in fifty. We optimise for permanence first, and commercial performance second — and we believe this ordering is also better business in the long run.

02
Measurement over narrative

We do not make claims we cannot substantiate with numbers. Our carbon removal is calculated using published methodologies, verified by third parties, and tied to specific batches of product. We would rather say less and be right than say more and be approximate.

03
Local material, global purpose

The feedstocks we work with are available across India. The problem we are working on is global. We are building in India not because it is easier, but because the opportunity — material, market, and talent — is here.

04
Carbon that earns its keep

We believe removal should not need to be charity. Every product we make does something useful for the person who buys it. The carbon removal is real. So is the value delivered. This is the model we are building toward at scale.
