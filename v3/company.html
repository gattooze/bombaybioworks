Built around carbon.

We are a carbon materials company producing biochar, activated carbon, and carbon fuel from agricultural residue. We are based in Maharashtra, India, and we are here because the opportunity was obvious and the infrastructure was missing.

The origin

The numbers were obvious. No one was doing it at scale. So we started.

India generates hundreds of millions of tonnes of agricultural residue every year. The scale of this material stream is enormous. Most of it is either left to decompose in the field, releasing carbon back into the atmosphere, or it is burned in the open, causing acute air quality damage.

At the same time, India imports significant volumes of activated carbon for water treatment. The market for clean solid fuel as a coal substitute exists wherever air quality regulations create cost pressure. And the global market for biochar carbon removal credits is growing rapidly as corporations and governments look for permanent CDR solutions.

The technology to convert agricultural residue into these three products simultaneously exists and has been proven at industrial scale. The feedstock is plentiful and currently a liability rather than an asset. The markets are real. We put all three together.

Why this

The problem we are solving.

Agricultural residue is a liability with an obvious fix.

When agricultural residue decomposes, it releases most of its stored carbon as CO2 and methane. When it is burned in the open, it releases all of it instantly, plus particulate matter and other pollutants. Neither outcome creates value. Thermal conversion creates value from the same material, captures the carbon permanently, and generates no ambient pollution.

India is the world's largest market for what we make.

India has the agricultural scale, the import dependence on activated carbon, the industrial coal demand, and the soil health challenge that collectively make our three products immediately relevant. The market is not theoretical. It is active, it is large, and it is underserved from domestic supply.

Carbon removal that actually removes carbon.

Most carbon offsetting involves payments for avoided future emissions, or carbon captured in living biomass that can be reversed. Biochar physically removes carbon from the carbon cycle and stores it in a form stable for over 3,000 years. This is not an offset. It is a removal. The distinction matters.

The business model does not depend on climate policy.

Activated carbon and carbon fuel are commercial products with established markets that exist independently of any carbon price or climate regulation. Biochar has both commercial agronomic demand and a CDR credit premium. The business works at zero carbon credit price and improves as those markets mature.

How we operate

Three principles.

01 Specification, not approximation.

We do not describe our products qualitatively. Every product is defined by a specification, tested to a standard, and shipped with a test certificate. We do not make claims we cannot back with a number and a method.

02 No waste from a waste feedstock.

Our process converts material that would otherwise have negative value (decomposing or burning residue) into products with positive value, and generates no additional waste streams in doing so. The synthesis gas powers the process. Nothing else leaves the site.

03 The climate claim is a fact, not a story.

We do not use climate language loosely. Our biochar has a measured H/C molar ratio, tested to EBC protocol. That ratio is the scientific basis for the permanence claim. Everything we say about carbon removal is backed by that number and the standard that defines it.
