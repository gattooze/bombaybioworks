Product 01

Biochar

Permanent carbon for agriculture, remediation, and CDR markets.

Biochar is a stable, carbon-rich solid produced by the thermal conversion of organic material under limited oxygen conditions. Unlike compost or organic mulch, which decompose and release their carbon within years, biochar is chemically and biologically resistant. Applied to soil, it stays for millennia.


Our biochar meets the European Biochar Certificate standard for carbon stability, with an H/C molar ratio below 0.4. This places it firmly within the EBC-defined permanence category, qualifying it for biochar carbon removal (BCR) credits under Puro.Earth, Verra, and equivalent carbon standard methodologies.


Beyond sequestration, biochar has measurable agronomic benefits: improved water retention, enhanced cation exchange capacity, and stimulated microbial activity in depleted soils.

Product 02

Activated Carbon

Ultraporous sorbent for purification, filtration, and separation.

Activated carbon is produced by controlled thermal activation of carbonised feedstock, developing an extraordinary internal surface area through the creation of millions of micropores. One gram of our activated carbon contains approximately 1,100 square metres of internal surface - available for adsorption at every point.


The structure is micropore-dominant, making it highly effective for the adsorption of small organic molecules, including pesticides, pharmaceuticals, disinfection by-products, and heavy metal species. This is the specification that water utilities, industrial plants, and pharmaceutical manufacturers specify.


We produce both granular activated carbon (GAC) and powdered activated carbon (PAC) to meet the full range of application requirements across gravity filtration, pressure systems, and dosing applications.

Primary applications
Water treatment Air purification Pharmaceutical purification Food and beverage decolorisation Industrial solvent recovery Gas separation

Product 03

Carbon Fuel

High fixed-carbon solid fuel with lower emissions than thermal coal.

Carbon fuel is the densified, high fixed-carbon co-product of our thermal conversion process. With fixed carbon content above 65 percent and total sulfur below 0.5 percent, it is a cleaner-burning, drop-in replacement for thermal coal in power generation, cement kilns, and industrial furnaces.


The calorific value is comparable to mid-grade thermal coal, which means existing combustion infrastructure can use it without modification. Sulfur oxide emissions are reduced by more than 60 percent relative to standard thermal coal. Ash content is lower, reducing post-combustion waste handling costs.


Carbon fuel is available as briquettes or granular material, sized to customer combustion system specifications. It is particularly suited for cement plants, steel mills, and combined heat-and-power (CHP) applications seeking to reduce their emission intensity without capital expenditure on new equipment.
