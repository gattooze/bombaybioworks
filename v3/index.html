What we make.

Three distinct materials from a single thermal conversion process. Each with its own market, specification, and application stack.

01 01 Biochar

Permanent carbon for agriculture and land remediation. Stable for millennia. EBC-certified and CDR-eligible.

<0.4 H/Corg Molar ratio — EBC permanence threshold
02 02 Activated Carbon

Ultraporous sorbent for water treatment, air purification, and industrial separation. Micropore-dominant structure.

1,100 m2/g BET surface area — ASTM D3663
03 03 Carbon Fuel

High fixed-carbon solid fuel. Drop-in replacement for thermal coal with significantly lower sulfur and ash content.

65%+ Fixed C Fixed carbon content — ASTM D3172

Our story

Built in Bombay. Built around carbon.

We started Bombay Bioworks because the numbers were obvious and no one in India was acting on them at scale. Agricultural residue streams here are enormous. The technology to convert them into high-value carbon materials exists. The markets for biochar, activated carbon, and clean solid fuel are real, growing, and underserved.

We put all three together. One facility. One continuous process. Three products, zero waste.
