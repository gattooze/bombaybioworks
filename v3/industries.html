01
Biochar

Agriculture and Soil Health

The original carbon soil amendment, rediscovered at scale.

Biochar applied to agricultural soil creates measurable improvements in water retention, nutrient holding capacity, and microbial activity. For Indian agriculture, which faces compounding challenges of declining soil organic matter, over-irrigation, and micronutrient depletion, biochar addresses all three simultaneously. Unlike chemical amendments, it does not leach, does not expire, and improves in effectiveness over the first several seasons as microbial colonisation of the pore structure increases.

  • Dryland farming water retention improvement
  • Micronutrient-depleted soil restoration
  • Paddy soil amendment post-harvest
  • Horticulture and cash crop soil conditioning
  • Carbon credit generation from soil application

India's total cultivated area with degraded or depleted topsoil. The largest single market for soil carbon amendments globally.

Activated Carbon

Water Treatment

The most widely specified technology for removing organic contaminants from water.

Activated carbon adsorption is the workhorse technology of municipal and industrial water treatment. It removes organic compounds, pesticides, disinfection by-products, pharmaceutical residues, and heavy metal complexes that other treatment steps cannot address. Granular activated carbon (GAC) is packed into fixed-bed pressure or gravity filters. Powdered activated carbon (PAC) is dosed directly into treatment streams. Both are consumables: spent carbon is either regenerated or replaced.

  • Municipal drinking water treatment (IS 877 grade)
  • Industrial effluent treatment before discharge
  • Groundwater remediation
  • Swimming pool and aquarium filtration
  • Point-of-use household filtration systems
03
Carbon Fuel

Energy and Industrial Heat

Lower emissions, same energy density. Without capital expenditure on new equipment.

Cement kilns, brick kilns, steel mills, and industrial boilers that run on thermal coal can switch partially or fully to carbon fuel without infrastructure modification. The calorific value is equivalent. Sulfur content is significantly lower, reducing SOx emissions and the associated regulatory and environmental burden. Ash content is also lower, reducing post-combustion waste. For industries facing air quality compliance pressure, carbon fuel provides a path to reduced emission intensity today.

  • Cement kiln fuel (partial or full substitution)
  • Brick and ceramics kiln firing
  • Industrial boiler fuel
  • Sponge iron and ferro-alloy production
  • Combined heat and power (CHP) plants
Market signal 740 MT/yr

India's annual thermal coal consumption in industrial applications (cement, brick, steel). Even a 1% substitution represents a very large addressable market.

Activated Carbon

Pharma and Food

The highest-margin application of activated carbon. Specification, not commodity.

Pharmaceutical-grade activated carbon is used in drug purification, decolorisation of APIs, and as a direct therapeutic agent (gastric decontamination). Food-grade activated carbon is used for decolorisation in sugar refining, glucose and starch production, beverage filtration, and edible oil purification. These applications require a higher purity standard and more precise specification than water-treatment grade, and they command a commensurately higher price.

  • API purification and decolorisation
  • Sugar and glucose refining
  • Edible oil and fat purification
  • Beverage filtration and clarity
  • Therapeutic/antidote applications
05
Biochar

Carbon Markets and CDR

Biochar carbon removal is one of the fastest-scaling carbon dioxide removal categories globally.

Biochar carbon removal (BCR) is a method of carbon dioxide removal that involves converting biomass into biochar and applying it to soil or other long-term storage settings. Each tonne of EBC-certified biochar applied represents approximately 2.2 tonnes of CO2 equivalent permanently removed from the carbon cycle. This removal can be monetised as a carbon credit under methodologies accepted by Puro.Earth, Verra, and other voluntary carbon standard bodies. The credit adds significant revenue to the core materials business, improving margins on biochar substantially.

  • Voluntary carbon market credit generation
  • Corporate net-zero pathway compliance
  • Article 6 CDR credits (national programmes)
  • Biochar carbon removal methodology credits (Puro.Earth)
Market signal $200+ /tCO₂

Prevailing BCR credit price on Puro.Earth for EBC-certified biochar carbon removal. Significantly above generic nature-based offset pricing.
