How we make it

Continuous.
Controlled.
Co-product.

Our thermal conversion process runs continuously, converting prepared agricultural feedstock into three distinct carbon products in a single pass. The process generates its own energy, requires no fossil fuel input, and produces no liquid or gaseous waste streams.

The principle

Carbon is not destroyed. It is reorganised.

Agricultural matter is primarily composed of cellulose, hemicellulose, and lignin. These compounds are carbon-rich. When heated in a controlled, oxygen-limited environment, they do not combust. They undergo thermochemical reorganisation.

The volatile fractions vaporise and are collected as synthesis gas and bio-oil. The solid residue, now almost entirely composed of stable carbon structures, is the base material from which we refine our three products. No waste leaves the site.

The four stages

From feedstock to finished carbon, continuously.

Stage by stage

What happens at each step.

01 Feedstock Preparation

Agricultural residue arrives in bulk. It is inspected for contaminants, shredded to a uniform particle size (typically 5 to 25 mm), and dried to a moisture content below 15 percent before entering the process.

Target moisture <15%
Particle size 5 to 25 mm
02 Thermal Conversion

Prepared feedstock moves continuously through the conversion unit. Temperature is precisely controlled between 400 and 700 degrees Celsius in a low-oxygen environment. The volatile organic fraction vaporises; the carbon skeleton remains as char.

Temperature range 400 to 700°C
Residence time 20 to 60 min
Oxygen level <2%
03 Separation and Grading

The hot char stream is cooled and separated. Fines are screened from granular and coarse fractions. Char destined for activation continues to the activation unit. Carbon fuel is densified into briquettes. Biochar is graded and sized for application.

Exit temperature <80°C
Char yield 25 to 35%
04 Finishing and QC

Each product undergoes batch quality testing before dispatch. Biochar is tested for H/C molar ratio, pH, and carbon content. Activated carbon is tested for BET surface area and iodine number. Carbon fuel undergoes proximate and calorific analysis.

Tests per batch 6 to 9
Turnaround 24 to 48 hr
Quality control

Tested to standard. Batch by batch.

Every batch of every product is tested against the relevant ASTM, EBC, or IS specification before dispatch. We do not ship on prior-batch data. If a batch fails, it does not leave.

Our in-house lab handles proximate analysis, moisture, ash, and pH. BET surface area and iodine number testing for activated carbon is handled by accredited external laboratories, with typical 24-hour turnaround.
