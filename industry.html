Enrichment

Carbon returned to the soil rather than sold as fuel. It changes how soil holds water and nutrients, and gives soil microbes a stable place to live.

~11%
average crop yield increase across 57 field and 59 pot experiments in 21 countries, with the largest gains in degraded tropical soils
