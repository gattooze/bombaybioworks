05 · bb-dyk  +  bb-offset-r  +  bb-float

Did you know, offset with a floating stat

Three things at once here. The panel is offset right rather than centred, a stat card floats off its corner on desktop, and a carbon network motif sits in the top right of the section. Below 900px the float becomes a normal stacked card.

Did you know

India has the largest area of bamboo on earth and takes about four percent of the world's bamboo trade. Meanwhile it imports activated carbon, most of it from China.

The raw material is already here. Very little of it is being turned into anything.

08 · bb-flow

Process flow

Numbered steps with connectors on desktop. Wraps to a grid, then to a stack, without the connectors ever pointing at nothing.

  1. 01FeedstockAgricultural residue and bamboo, graded on arrival.
