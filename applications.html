01 — Earth

Returned to soil.
Locked for centuries.

When stabilised carbon is applied to soil, it does two things simultaneously: it improves the soil and it stays there. Unlike conventional soil amendments that break down within seasons, fixed carbon resists biological decomposition for hundreds of years.

The result is soil that holds more water, supports more microbial life, and retains more nutrients — while keeping carbon out of the atmosphere for centuries. Agriculture and carbon removal become the same act.

02 — Fire

The same energy.
A different origin.

India's industrial sector runs on combustion. Boilers, kilns, furnaces — they need dense, reliable, consistent fuel. We produce carbon that delivers equivalent or superior energy performance to fossil alternatives, from a biological and traceable source.

The carbon we combust was drawn from the atmosphere by growing plants. When it burns, it returns to the atmosphere — but the net addition over the cycle is close to zero. Unlike fossil carbon, which was locked underground for millions of years and has no modern atmospheric counterpart.

Activated carbon — carbon whose internal surface area has been vastly expanded through a secondary process — is one of the most effective purification materials known. A single gram contains surface area measured in hundreds of square metres.

We produce activated carbon from our biological feedstock. Its adsorption capacity, pore structure, and surface chemistry make it suitable for the most demanding purification applications — in water, in air, in cosmetics, and in specialty industrial processes.
