The science

Why fixed carbon
is different.

Not all carbon removal is equal. The difference between temporary storage and permanent sequestration is centuries — and we work only in the permanent kind.

How we convert it →
01

Biological carbon is short-cycle by nature.

Plants and biomass absorb atmospheric CO₂ as they grow. But when they decompose or burn, that carbon returns to the atmosphere within years. The cycle repeats — and the net removal is close to zero.

The only way to break the cycle is to interrupt the decomposition step and lock the carbon in a stable, non-reactive form.

02

Stabilised carbon resists decomposition for centuries.

When biological material is converted through a controlled thermochemical process, the carbon structure transforms into a highly stable form that resists biological breakdown. Instead of decades, the carbon stays fixed for hundreds to thousands of years.

03

What we produce is carbon that does work while it stays fixed.

The goal is not just to remove carbon — it is to remove it in a form that creates value in the process. Our carbon streams serve real industrial, agricultural, and purification purposes. Removal and utility are not in tension. They're the same thing.

How we measure

Our tracking
methodology.

We do not rely on estimates or offsets. Our carbon accounting is built on mass balance, direct measurement, and third-party verification at each stage of the process.

Mass balance
accounting

We measure feedstock inputs and carbon outputs at each stage. The mass of carbon entering the process is tracked against every output stream — soil carbon, combustion carbon, purification carbon, and process gases. No carbon is unaccounted for.

Third-party
verification

Independent auditors verify our measurement methodology, feedstock documentation, and output specifications. Every tonne we claim removed is a tonne that has been examined by eyes that are not ours.

Digital carbon
ledger

Every production run generates a digital record — feedstock batch, conversion parameters, output yield, carbon grade, and end-use destination. The ledger is the source of truth. It does not round up.

Permanence classes

Not all removal
lasts equally.

We produce carbon in multiple permanence classes — from centuries-long soil sequestration to industrial-grade combustion that displaces fossil carbon in this year's energy balance. Both matter. Both are tracked separately.

Soil-grade stabilised carbon achieves the longest permanence — measurable in centuries rather than years. This is the gold standard of carbon removal.
