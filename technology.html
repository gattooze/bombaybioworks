How we do it

Precision.
At every stage.

The conversion of biological material into graded carbon products is a controlled, measurable process. Every input is assessed. Every output is specified. The conditions in between are the difference between a soil amendment and a fuel.

Process

Four stages.
Full control.

Every batch moves through a defined sequence. The conditions at each stage determine the properties of the output. Temperature, residence time, feedstock quality, oxygen environment — each variable is monitored and logged.

01
Feedstock assessment and preparation
Incoming biological material is assessed for moisture content, ash level, density, and elemental composition. Only material meeting our input specification is processed. Oversized material is reduced. Moisture is brought within an optimal range before entering the conversion stage.
Input quality
+
02
Thermochemical conversion
Feedstock undergoes controlled thermal decomposition in a limited-oxygen environment. Temperature, residence time, and heating rate are precisely managed throughout the run. The chemical structure of the input material is broken down and reorganised — volatile compounds are driven off; stable carbon structures form in the solid fraction. Process gases are captured and utilised.
Core process
+
03
Grading and quality testing
The solid carbon output is sampled at discharge. Key parameters — fixed carbon content, volatile matter, ash, moisture, surface area, and pH — are measured and compared against grade specifications. Batches meeting the soil-grade specification are separated from those meeting fuel-grade or purification-grade thresholds. Off-spec material is re-processed or blended.
Quality control
+
04
Tracking, logging, and dispatch
Every passing batch is assigned a unique identifier linking it to feedstock origin, conversion run parameters, and quality test results. This chain of custody follows the product to the end customer. The data forms the basis for third-party verification of carbon removal claims and customer sustainability reporting.

Output specifications

Three grades.
Defined by purpose.

01
Soil Grade
Stable carbon for permanent soil application

High fixed-carbon fraction, optimised pore structure, and neutral-alkaline pH. Designed to persist in the soil for centuries while improving water retention and supporting soil biology.

Fixed carbon ≥ 70%
pH range 7.0 – 9.5
Permanence 500+ years
Application Agriculture, sequestration
02
Fuel Grade
Dense, consistent combustion carbon

High calorific value, low ash, low moisture. Engineered to perform as a direct substitute for fossil-derived fuel in industrial applications — boilers, kilns, furnaces, and co-firing.

Calorific value 4,500+ kcal/kg
Moisture ≤ 8%
Ash content ≤ 10%
Application Industry, export, co-firing
03
Purification Grade
Activated carbon for adsorption applications

Maximised surface area through controlled activation. The expanded micropore structure enables high adsorption capacity for contaminants, impurities, and target compounds.

Surface area 900+ m²/g
Iodine number 900+ mg/g
Forms Powder, granular, pellet
Application Water, air, cosmetics, refining
