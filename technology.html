How we work

From biomass
to fixed carbon.

Four defined stages. Measurable inputs and outputs at each one. The conditions between them determine what you get at the end — and what you can claim about it.

The four stages

Controlled at every step.

Temperature, residence time, oxygen level, feedstock quality — these are not background variables. They determine whether the output is biochar, activated carbon, or fuel. We control them deliberately and log them for every batch.

01
Feedstock assessment
Input quality
+

Incoming biological material is tested for moisture content, ash level, elemental composition, and physical density before entering the process. Material outside our input specification is rejected or returned. Moisture is adjusted to the correct range. Oversized material is reduced. The conversion stage only sees feedstock that meets our intake criteria.

02
Thermal conversion
Core process
+

Feedstock is heated in a low-oxygen environment. Temperature, heating rate, and residence time are set based on the target output — higher temperatures and longer residence times yield higher fixed carbon content and lower volatile matter. Process gases are captured and either used for heat recovery or measured and vented. Solid output is cooled before discharge.

03
Grading and quality testing
Quality control
+

Solid output is sampled at discharge. Fixed carbon, volatile matter, ash content, moisture, pH, and — for activated carbon — surface area and iodine number are measured and compared against grade thresholds. Batches meeting soil-grade specification are separated from fuel-grade and purification-grade outputs. Off-spec material is reprocessed or blended to the nearest qualifying grade.

04
Batch record and dispatch
Provenance
+

Each passing batch is assigned a unique identifier linking it to its feedstock intake record, conversion run log, and quality test results. This record accompanies the product to the customer and forms the basis for third-party verification and carbon removal reporting. The identifier does not expire. The record does not change after issue.
