What this is

Most carbon removal is a spreadsheet. A tonne of biochar in the ground is not.

We make physical things — products that can be tested, weighed, applied, and verified. Biochar in soil doesn't drift back into the atmosphere. Activated carbon in a filtration system is doing a job. Dense carbon fuel displacing coal is a displacement that happened.

These are not offsets. They're objects. Made from agricultural residue. In India. At industrial scale.
