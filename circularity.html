Where the material actually comes from

Agricultural residue does not appear from nowhere. It comes from agrarian and tribal communities who currently have little use for it beyond burning it in the field.

Buying that residue at a fair price, rather than treating it as waste to be disposed of, is the starting point of the entire supply chain.

Verified against a real standard

The European Biochar Certificate was the first system built specifically to certify biochar as a carbon sink, covering feedstock sourcing, production emissions, and storage. Digital monitoring, reporting, and verification track a batch from intake through pyrolysis to application, with location tagged records rather than a claim taken on trust.
