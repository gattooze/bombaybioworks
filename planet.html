The process

A closed loop, not an open one

  1. 01Residue collectedMaterial that would otherwise be burnt in the open or left to rot.
  2. 02CarbonisedConverted without venting the gas produced. That gas fuels the next batch.
  3. 03Heat recoveredActivation heat is captured rather than exhausted to the air.
  4. 04Nothing wastedEvery fraction of the process has a use, none of it is discarded.

Every fraction, put to use

Pyrolysis does not produce one output. It produces carbon, gas, and heat, and all three are used rather than vented. The gas fires the next batch. The heat is recovered. Only the carbon itself leaves the facility, as product.
